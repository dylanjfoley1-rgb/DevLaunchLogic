# DevLaunchLogic

My app helps developers decide to launch a free-to-play or pay-to-play game by analyzing player retention and transaction conversion rates across games of similar genres, pricing, or through their own playtests/demos

> A course project for **MIST 4600: Computer Programming in Business** at the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business (Fall 2026).

## Problem
Developers and project owners must determine whether a new game should choose a free-to-play or pay-to-play model early in production to support long-term retention and app growth. Making this decision carries high risks and is vulnerable to costly miscalculations of market signals. Creators that decide on a revenue generation strategy that conflicts with player behavior risks underperforming revenue and wasted development.

## What it does
My app answers three questions:
1. **Cost:** What do games in the studio's genre cost, and has that changed over the years?
2. **Retention Rate:** What is the project retention rate for this genre?
3. **Conversion Rate:** How many customers show interest in purchasing after testing a game demo? (demo to wishlist)

## Data
The [Steam Games Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/artermiloff/steam-games-dataset) on Kaggle, collected in March 2025. The dataset is shared under the MIT License by its creator, artermiloff. This repo uses `steam_games.csv`, a smaller file made for the course: the 1,000 games with the most reviews.
